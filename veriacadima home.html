How a record earns the Verified label

Nothing is public just because it was uploaded. Each step has a named reviewer and a saved history.

  1. Verify the universityPlatform reviewers check evidence and domain ownership before a university goes live.
  2. Approve the memberA university administrator approves each researcher. Email eligibility alone is not enough.
  3. Review the recordA reviewer checks affiliation, duplicates and rights before a paper or patent becomes active.
  4. Publish or protectPublic metadata is searchable. Protected files need an access request that the university can approve or refuse.

Built for institutions that need to answer for their data

Each university runs as its own isolated space. Data, files, search and reports never cross from one university to another unless a grant says so.

One publication is stored once and linked to every university that legitimately claims it, so duplicates and conflicting affiliations do not pile up.
